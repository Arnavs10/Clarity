RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on the 1st day of September 2026 between the Lessor and the Lessee.

1. TERM
The premises are let for a period of eleven (11) months commencing 1 September 2026.

2. RENT AND ESCALATION
The Lessee shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000 payable on or before the 5th of each month. The rent shall escalate by fifteen percent (15%) upon each renewal.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Lessee shall deposit a sum equal to six (6) months rent as an interest free security deposit. The said deposit shall be refunded within ninety (90) days of the Lessee vacating the premises, subject to deductions at the sole discretion of the Lessor.

4. LOCK IN PERIOD
The Lessee agrees to a lock in period of nine (9) months. Should the Lessee vacate prior to the expiry of the said lock in period, the entire security deposit shall stand forfeited.

5. NOTICE
The Lessor may terminate this Agreement by giving fifteen (15) days notice. The Lessee shall give three (3) months notice in writing.

6. ENTRY AND INSPECTION
The Lessor or his authorised representative shall be entitled to enter the premises at any reasonable time for the purpose of inspection.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
